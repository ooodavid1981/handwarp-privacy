Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 (effective: November 19, 2026)

"HandWarp" (the "App") uses your device's front camera to recognize your hands so you can steer a rocket in the air. No account is needed to play. Camera images are analyzed only on your device in real time; they are not stored and not sent to anyone. A small amount of data is sent to a server only when you view the online (two-hand) leaderboard, upload a two-hand 60-second score, report another player, or delete your leaderboard data.

1. Summary

  • Camera images are processed only on your device: no recording, no photos, nothing stored or uploaded.
  • No accounts, no ads, no third-party analytics or tracking tools, no in-app purchases.
  • The online leaderboard accepts only two-hand gesture scores (touch and keyboard can be used to play, but those scores are not uploaded); it publicly shows the nickname you choose, your score, flight distance, rings passed, time, warp level and a public player ID; please do not use your real name or anything that identifies you.
  • Our database keeps only a salted hash of your IP address, cleared after 30 days; however, our server provider's connection logs temporarily hold the raw IP for up to 1 day (see Section 3).
  • Your personal-best scores and nickname are kept until you delete them; you can do so in the App under Settings > Delete my leaderboard data.

2. What data the App uses on your device

  • Front camera images: The App uses the front camera to recognize the position and pose of your hands (fist, open hand, the tilt of your two hands) so you can play without touching the screen. On iPhone this is done frame by frame, in real time, on the device by Apple's built-in Vision framework; the web version uses MediaPipe inside the browser, also on the device. The App does not record video, does not take photos, and does not store or upload any images.
  • Hand-recognition values: values such as hand joint coordinates (not images) are kept only temporarily in memory, used to interpret your controls in real time, and discarded.
  • Game progress and settings: level progress, best scores, language and settings, plus the random player ID and secret used for the leaderboard, are stored in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read.
  • Apart from the leaderboard data described in Section 3, none of this leaves your device.

3. Data collected for the online leaderboard and reports

The App connects only when you: open the online (two-hand) leaderboard (including when the result screen of a two-hand 60-second challenge loads your rank); upload a two-hand 60-second score; report a player; or delete your own leaderboard data. Everything can be played without a connection. When you view the board, the App sends its random player ID so your own row can be marked; the server does not store it separately for this. The following is the notice required by data protection laws.

  • Collector: MRJC (an individual developer, "we").
  • What we collect (when you submit a score): the nickname you choose (2 to 16 characters), score, flight distance, time taken, rings passed, warp level and control type (the online board accepts only two-hand gestures, so this is always two-hand), the time of the score; a random player ID generated by the App (it is not your Apple account or device serial number, and changes on reinstall); a player secret (the server stores only its SHA-256 hash, to confirm that scores come from the same player); and the IP address of your connection (our database stores only a salted hash, to prevent abuse and score cheating).
  • What we collect (when you report a player): a hash identifying you and a hash of your IP address (no player profile is created for the reporter), a deletion code derived from your player secret (so someone who only reported and never submitted a score can still ask for deletion by email), the reported player, the reason, a snapshot of the reported nickname and score, and the time. Report data is linked to your identifier hash.
  • Purpose: to show and rank the leaderboard; to prevent cheating and abuse; to handle reports and keep the community safe. Nothing else; not for advertising or marketing.
  • What is public: the leaderboard API returns to anyone: nickname, score, flight distance, rings passed, time taken, warp level, the time of the score and a public player ID. Your player secret, IP hash, deletion code and report data are not public.
  • Retention, item by item:
  • Per-run scores: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Personal-best scores and nickname: kept until you delete them (you can do so in the App).
  • IP address hashes: cleared after 30 days.
  • Rejected submissions (the player ID, reason and the submitted values kept when a score fails a plausibility or rate check): deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Reports: deleted automatically after 180 days.
  • Data needed for automatic hiding: when a nickname is reported, the system uses the report contents and a salted hash of the network identifier to tell whether several different players reported it, and hides the nickname automatically; the network identifier is kept for at most 30 days and reports for 180 days.
  • Report notifications: contain numbers only and are sent to the developer through GitHub; they do not include nicknames, scores or any other content.
  • Hide-reason note: while a player is hidden, the note we write about why is stored on that player's data (not public); it is deleted when the player is unhidden or deletes their data.
  • Moderation log (hides, unhides, dismissals and deletions we performed: the action, a hash of the player ID, the time, and a note we wrote such as the hide reason or a count): deleted automatically after 180 days.
  • Deletion record: left only when a player who is currently hidden, banned or whose nickname is masked deletes their data. It holds a hash of the player ID, the sanction flag, and the times of the sanction (including the nickname mask and its review) and the deletion (to stop a sanctioned player from evading it by deleting and re-registering); no nickname, scores or notes. A player who was not sanctioned leaves no such record. It has no fixed retention period: it is kept until the same player ID submits a score again (the sanction is moved back to that player and the record is deleted) or until we remove it manually. The moderation log above is kept 180 days separately.
  • Server provider logs: Supabase's API connection logs temporarily hold the raw IP address for up to 1 day (current plan; if the plan changes we will update this policy first). These logs are controlled by the provider and we look at them only to troubleshoot.
  • Location and recipients: database data is stored in Supabase's data center in Tokyo, Japan (ap-northeast-1); report notifications (numbers only) are sent to the developer through GitHub (United States). Both act on our behalf, only on our instructions, and not for any other use. We do not give data to advertisers, data brokers or other third parties.
  • Nickname filter: nicknames are filtered for profanity, URLs and phone numbers; we may change or remove an inappropriate nickname and remove its scores.
  • If you do not take part: nothing else in the App is affected.

4. Your rights and how to use them

You may at any time ask to access, obtain a copy of, correct, stop the collection or use of, or delete your data.

  • Deletion: use Settings > Delete my leaderboard data in the App; your nickname, all scores and the reports you sent are deleted immediately. Deleting does not change the random player ID on your phone; reinstalling the App does. If you cannot use the App, email mrjc0509@gmail.com with the deletion code shown on the App's settings page (only you can see it); we will handle it within 30 days after receiving and verifying the request, and can delete your scores, nickname and the reports you sent. Without the deletion code we cannot confirm that you are the person concerned, so we cannot process the request.
  • Other requests: email mrjc0509@gmail.com; there is no charge.
  • Please note: we can only find your data by the deletion code. It is lost when you delete the App, so please use the in-app deletion, or note the code, before deleting the App.
  • Reporting: if you see an inappropriate nickname or a cheated score, use Leaderboard > tap the player's entry > Report in the App; you can also block that player (blocking is stored only on your phone). When a player's nickname is reported within 30 days by at least three players who have played and who are on three different networks, the system automatically hides that player's nickname first (the leaderboard then shows a default name; the score is not affected), and the developer then reviews it manually. The mask applies to the player, not to one nickname, so it stays on after a name change until the developer has reviewed it. When there are pending reports, the developer receives a daily notification. We never remove a player from the leaderboard automatically just because of reports.
  • Users in the EU, UK and similar regions also have the right to complain to their local data protection authority. The legal basis for processing is providing the leaderboard service you asked for, and our legitimate interest in preventing abuse.

5. Storage and security

  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS); our database stores only hashes of your IP address and player secret, not the raw values (for the provider's temporary IP logs see Section 3).
  • Data on your device: deleting the App also deletes the data it stored on the device. If iCloud Backup or a computer backup is on, the backup may include the App's progress and settings; it is kept by your iCloud account or computer and we cannot access it.
  • If a data breach occurs, we will notify affected people as required by law.

6. What the App does not do

  • It does not store or upload camera images.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • It does not sell or give your data to third parties for advertising or marketing.
  • If you turned on "Share With App Developers" in your iPhone's Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide the developer with diagnostics such as crash logs, handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.

7. Permissions

  • Camera (for gesture control): can be turned off at any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera; you can still play with the on-screen touch buttons.
  • Network: used only by the online leaderboard (viewing the board, uploading two-hand scores, reporting, deleting your own data).
  • Haptic feedback (vibration): needs no separate permission in iOS; it can be turned off in settings.

8. Children

The App is not designed specifically for children under 13. Leaderboard nicknames are shown publicly, so parents should tell children not to use real names; to have a minor's data deleted, use the in-app deletion or a parent can email mrjc0509@gmail.com (with the deletion code). Apart from the leaderboard and reports, the App does not collect anyone's personal data, including children's.
