Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026 (effective: November 24, 2026)

"HandWarp" (the "App") uses your device's front camera to recognize your hands so you can steer a rocket in the air. No account is needed to play. Camera images are analyzed only on your device in real time; they are not stored and not sent to anyone. A small amount of data is sent to a server only when you view the online (two-hand) leaderboard, upload a two-hand 60-second score, report another player, delete your data, do the daily check-in (see Section 3), send anonymous usage statistics (daily totals; can be turned off in Settings, see Section 5), or choose to send a bug report in the App (see Section 4).

1. Summary

  • Camera images are processed only on your device: no recording, no photos, nothing stored or uploaded.
  • No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no integrated third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs (the App uses the open-source Capacitor framework).
  • A bug report is sent only when you tap "Send": the category you choose, the description you write, the app version and (optionally, you can turn it off) device model, iOS version, language, control mode and recent error codes; the App does not automatically attach your name, email, photos, video or hand coordinates (please do not put personal data in the description), the report is not linked to your leaderboard identity, and it is deleted automatically after 30 days (see Section 4).
  • Anonymous usage statistics: only daily totals (for example how many runs started or finished), the App version and the platform; no nickname, player ID, device identifier, images or hand data, and no cookies; kept for 2 years; you can turn it off in Settings > Anonymous usage stats (see Section 5).
  • The online leaderboard accepts only two-hand gesture scores (touch and keyboard can be used to play, but those scores are not uploaded); it publicly shows the nickname you choose, your rank, score, flight distance, rings passed, warp level, the time of the score and a public player ID (Warp 1, 2 and 3 share one board, with one best score per player per warp); please do not use your real name or anything that identifies you.
  • Leaderboard input records: only when you upload a 60-second challenge score, the steering, throttle and brake values of every step of that run are sent along with the score (no images, no hand coordinates). The server uses them to recalculate and verify the score, to prevent cheating and for manual review. For each board only the best run of the top 100 is kept, for at most 30 days; other runs keep only a score summary (see Section 3).
  • Our database keeps only a salted hash of your IP address, cleared after 30 days; however, our server provider's connection logs temporarily hold the raw IP for up to 1 day (see Section 3).
  • Your personal-best scores and nickname are kept until you delete them, or deleted automatically 180 days after your last check-in or score upload; you can delete them yourself in the App under Settings > Delete all my data.

2. What data the App uses on your device

  • Front camera images: The App uses the front camera to recognize the position and pose of your hands (fist, open hand, the tilt of your two hands) so you can play without touching the screen. On iPhone this is done frame by frame, in real time, on the device by Apple's built-in Vision framework; the web version uses MediaPipe inside the browser, also on the device. The App does not record video, does not take photos, and does not store or upload any images.
  • Hand-recognition values: values such as hand joint coordinates (not images) are kept only temporarily in memory, used to interpret your controls in real time, and discarded.
  • Game progress and settings: level progress, best scores, language and settings, plus the random player ID and secret used for the leaderboard, are stored in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read.
  • Pending input records: the input record of a two-hand 60-second challenge run (steering, throttle and brake values; no images or hand coordinates) is kept temporarily in the App's private storage until it is uploaded or rejected by the server, at most 10 records; Delete all my data clears them too.
  • Apart from the leaderboard data (Section 3), any bug report you choose to send (Section 4) and the anonymous daily totals (Section 5), none of this leaves your device.

3. Data collected for the online leaderboard and reports

The App connects only when you: open the online (two-hand) leaderboard (including when the result screen of a two-hand 60-second challenge loads your rank); upload a two-hand 60-second score; report a player; delete your own data; do the daily check-in; send anonymous usage statistics (at most once a day, see Section 5); or send a bug report you choose to submit (see Section 4). Everything can be played without a connection. If this device has uploaded a score (so the server holds your data), it checks in about once a day: when more than 20 hours have passed since the last check-in and you open the App or bring it back to the foreground with a connection, it sends the random player ID and the player secret used to verify it, and nothing else; this tells us whether you are still using the App, so that your data can be deleted automatically after long disuse. A device that has only viewed the leaderboard, only filed reports, or never connected does not check in. When you view the board, the App sends its random player ID so your own row can be marked; the server does not store it separately for this. The following is the notice required by data protection laws.

  • Collector: MRJC (an individual developer, "we").
  • What we collect (when you submit a score): the nickname you choose (2 to 16 characters), score, flight distance, time taken, rings passed, warp level and control type (the online board accepts only two-hand gestures, so this is always two-hand), the time of the score; a random player ID generated by the App (it is not your Apple account or device serial number, and changes on reinstall); a player secret (the server stores only its SHA-256 hash, to confirm that scores come from the same player); and the IP address of your connection (our database stores only a salted hash, to prevent abuse and score cheating).
  • What we collect (when you report a player): a hash identifying you and a hash of your IP address (no player profile is created for the reporter), an identifier hash derived from your player secret (only the hash is stored; it recognizes that reports come from the same reporter and cannot be reversed into your secret), the reported player, the reason, a snapshot of the reported nickname and score, and the time. Report data is linked to your identifier hash.
  • What we collect (daily check-in): the random player ID generated by the App and the player secret used to verify it (the server only compares it and stores only its hash), so that the server records the time of the last check-in for that player ID; nothing else. Our database does not store the IP address of a check-in (for the provider's connection logs see "Server provider logs" below).
  • What we collect (leaderboard input records, only when you upload a 60-second challenge score): the steering, throttle and brake values of every step of that run (about 60 steps per second of play, roughly 11 KB per run), together with the rules version and App version; no camera images, no hand coordinates or any hand-recognition values. From these values the server also derives "input feature statistics" (for example how throttle and brake are switched); those statistics do not contain the player ID.
  • Purpose: to show and rank the leaderboard; to prevent cheating and abuse; to handle reports and keep the community safe; to tell whether you are still using the App (the daily check-in) so that data can be deleted automatically after long disuse. Nothing else; not for advertising or marketing.
  • Purpose (leaderboard input records): so that the server can recalculate and verify your score (the server's result is the score that counts), prevent cheating and, where necessary, allow manual review by the developer; the input feature statistics are used to judge the way of control (gestures or keyboard). Nothing else.
  • What is public: the leaderboard API returns to anyone: rank, nickname, score, flight distance, rings passed, warp level, the time of the score and a public player ID (Warp 1, 2 and 3 share one board; the time taken for a run is not public). Your player secret, IP hash, identifier hash and report data are not public.
  • Retention, item by item:
  • Per-run scores: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Leaderboard input records: only the best run of each board's top 100 is kept, for at most 30 days, then deleted automatically; other runs keep only a score summary (score, distance, rings passed, etc.) and no input record. When you use Delete all my data, your input records on the server are deleted too, and the pending or last-run record on this device is cleared.
  • Input feature statistics: do not contain the player ID; deleted automatically after 180 days; because they cannot be tied to a person they are not deleted individually by Delete all my data.
  • Personal-best scores and nickname: kept until you delete them (you can do so in the App), or deleted automatically 180 days after your last check-in or score upload.
  • Daily check-in record: the server keeps only the time of the last check-in for your player ID; our database does not store the IP address of a check-in (for the provider's connection logs see "Server provider logs" below); it is kept and deleted together with your player data.
  • Automatic deletion after long inactivity: more than 180 days after your last check-in or last successful score upload (for player data that never checked in or uploaded, counted from the day it was created), a daily server cleanup job deletes it; the scope is the same as when you delete it yourself: your nickname, all scores, the reports you sent, the reports made against you, rejected-submission records, leaderboard input records and the player data. A "Deletion record" (below) remains only if, at the moment of deletion, you are still hidden, banned or have a masked nickname; the moderation log is not part of the deletion and has its own 180-day retention, after which it is deleted automatically. Once you delete the App it no longer checks in, so the data is deleted automatically after 180 days; as long as you keep the App and open it with a connection at least once within 180 days, it checks in and the retention is extended.
  • IP address hashes: cleared after 30 days.
  • Rejected submissions (the player ID, reason and the submitted values kept when a score fails a plausibility or rate check): deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Reports: deleted automatically after 180 days.
  • Data needed for automatic hiding: when a nickname is reported, the system uses the report contents and a salted hash of the network identifier to tell whether several different players made unresolved nickname reports (reports for cheating or other reasons, and reports already handled, do not count), and hides the nickname automatically; the network identifier is kept for at most 30 days and reports for 180 days.
  • Report notifications: contain numbers only and are sent to the developer through GitHub; they do not include nicknames, scores or any other content.
  • Hide-reason note: while a player is hidden, the note we write about why is stored on that player's data (not public); it is deleted when the player is unhidden or deletes their data.
  • Moderation log (hides, unhides, dismissals and deletions we performed: the action, a hash of the player ID, the time, and a note we wrote such as the hide reason or a count): deleted automatically after 180 days.
  • Deletion record: left only when a player who, at the moment of deletion, is hidden, banned or has a masked nickname is deleted (whether by themselves, by an administrator or automatically). It holds a hash of the player ID, the sanction flag, and the times of the sanction (including the nickname mask and its review) and the deletion (to stop a sanctioned player from evading it by deleting and re-registering); no nickname, scores or notes. A player who was not sanctioned leaves no such record. It has no fixed retention period: it is kept until the same player ID submits a score again (the sanction is moved back to that player and the record is deleted) or until we remove it manually. The moderation log above is kept 180 days separately.
  • Server provider logs: Supabase's API connection logs temporarily hold the raw IP address for up to 1 day (current plan; if the plan changes we will update this policy first). These logs are controlled by the provider and we look at them only to troubleshoot.
  • Location and recipients: database data is stored in Supabase's data center in Tokyo, Japan (ap-northeast-1); report notifications (numbers only) are sent to the developer through GitHub (United States). Both act on our behalf, only on our instructions, and not for any other use. We do not give data to advertisers, data brokers or other third parties.
  • Nickname filter: nicknames are filtered for profanity, URLs and phone numbers; we may change or remove an inappropriate nickname and remove its scores.
  • If you do not take part: nothing else in the App is affected.

4. Bug reports

You can report a problem from the App under Settings > Feedback > Report a problem. A report is sent only when you tap "Send"; the App never reports automatically.

  • What you choose and write yourself: the problem category, and whatever you type in the description (up to 500 characters, optional). The description is free text, so please do not write your name, email, phone number or other personal data; both the App and the server try to replace text in the description that looks like an email address or a phone number / run of 9 or more digits with [email] or [number] (this cannot be guaranteed to catch everything), so please do not put personal data in the description.
  • Fields the App attaches automatically: the app version; and, when "Include technical info" is on, your device model, iOS version, interface language, control mode (air gestures, touch or keyboard) and recent error codes (up to 10, codes and times only, for example camera permission denied; never error message text). "Include technical info" is on by default and you can turn it off (the App remembers your choice); when off, only the category, description and app version are sent. Before sending you can see every field that will actually be sent under "See what will be sent".
  • Not actively collected: name, email, phone number, photos, video, hand coordinates; the App does not attach these automatically.
  • Not linked to your leaderboard identity: a bug report carries no player ID or player secret, so we cannot match a report to any player on the leaderboard.
  • IP address: used only to limit abuse (how many reports one network can send per hour and per day); our database keeps only a salted hash made specifically for bug reports (it uses a separate salt from the leaderboard data and is never matched against leaderboard data) and clears it within 48 hours; if the server cannot see a trusted network address, the report is not accepted. The server provider's connection logs are described in Section 3 under "Server provider logs".
  • Purpose: only to find and fix problems; not used for advertising or marketing and not given to third parties. Only the developer can read reports, after signing in to the admin page.
  • Retention: reports are deleted automatically after 30 days. When the developer deletes a report in the admin page, its description and technical info are cleared immediately; the remaining fields (time, category, app version) are kept until the 30-day limit.
  • Access and deletion: because a report is not linked to your identity, we cannot find a specific report on request; it is deleted automatically after 30 days.

5. Anonymous usage statistics

To learn whether anyone is playing and which parts people reach, the App sends daily totals. You can turn this off at any time in the App under Settings > Anonymous usage stats; after that nothing is counted or sent, and any counts still waiting on your phone are erased.

  • What is sent: the total number of times per day of each event, such as app opens, runs started, runs finished (by journey, 60-second challenge, practice or tutorial), arrivals at each journey station, replays of the same station, and how many runs used each control type (hand gestures, touch or keyboard), plus the App version, the platform (iOS or web) and the date (UTC).
  • What is not sent: no nickname, player ID, player secret, device identifier, advertising identifier, camera images, hand data, location or any personal data; no cookies are used. Each record is just a number: "on this day, in this version, this event happened this many times"; no record can be matched to a person or a phone.
  • When it is sent: counts are first added up on your phone and sent at most once a day: when you open or return to the App with a connection and earlier days have unsent counts, the totals of those days are sent (at most 7 days at a time; older ones are dropped); today's counts wait until tomorrow. Each count is recorded on the day it happened (UTC date), not on the day it arrives; the server accepts only dates within the last 7 days, and anything older is not accepted. A failed send is kept for the next try.
  • IP address: our database does not store an IP address for this feature (the provider's connection logs are covered in Section 3 under "Server provider logs").
  • Purpose: only to understand how many people play, how far they get and where they get stuck, so we can improve the game; not for advertising or marketing, and not shared with third parties.
  • Retention: daily totals are kept for 2 years and then deleted automatically by the server's daily clean-up job. Because they contain no personal data, we cannot find or delete the counts of one specific person on request.
  • No effect on play: everything works the same with it turned off; builds without a server connection configured (for example offline web testing) send nothing.

6. Your rights and how to use them

You may at any time ask to access, obtain a copy of, correct, stop the collection or use of, or delete your data.

  • Deletion: use Settings > Delete all my data in the App. If you have leaderboard data, a connection is needed: the server is asked to delete your nickname, all scores, the reports you sent, the reports made against you, rejected-submission records, leaderboard input records and the player data (the moderation log is not included and has its own 180-day retention); only after you are connected and the server has confirmed the deletion is the game data on this device (level progress, best scores, settings, etc.) cleared. Deleting does not change the random player ID on your phone; reinstalling the App does. The App needs no deletion code and has none.
  • If you have already deleted the App: we can identify the data on the server only by the random player ID inside the App, and we cannot confirm from a nickname or a score which entry is yours, so if you email mrjc0509@gmail.com we will reply with an explanation but cannot delete anything on the basis of an email. The data is deleted automatically 180 days after your last check-in or score upload.
  • Other requests: email mrjc0509@gmail.com; there is no charge.
  • Reporting: if you see an inappropriate nickname or a cheated score, use Leaderboard > tap the player's entry > Report in the App; you can also block that player (blocking is stored only on your phone). When a player's nickname receives unresolved nickname reports within 30 days from at least three players who have played and who are on three different networks, the system automatically hides that player's nickname first (the leaderboard then shows a default name; the score is not affected), and the developer then reviews it manually. The mask applies to the player, not to one nickname, so it stays on after a name change until the developer has reviewed it. When there are pending reports, the developer receives a daily notification. We never remove a player from the leaderboard automatically just because of reports.
  • Users in the EU, UK and similar regions also have the right to complain to their local data protection authority. The legal basis for processing is providing the leaderboard service you asked for, and our legitimate interest in preventing abuse.

7. Storage and security

  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS); our database stores only hashes of your IP address and player secret, not the raw values (for the provider's temporary IP logs see Section 3).
  • Data on your device: deleting the App also deletes the data it stored on the device. If iCloud Backup or a computer backup is on, the backup may include the App's progress and settings; it is kept by your iCloud account or computer and we cannot access it.
  • If a data breach occurs, we will notify affected people as required by law.

8. What the App does not do

  • It does not store or upload camera images.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no integrated third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs (the App uses the open-source Capacitor framework).
  • It does not sell or give your data to third parties for advertising or marketing.
  • If you turned on "Share With App Developers" in your iPhone's Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide the developer with diagnostics such as crash logs, handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.

9. Permissions

  • Camera (for gesture control): can be turned off at any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera; you can still play with the on-screen touch buttons.
  • Network: used only by the online leaderboard, the anonymous usage statistics (can be turned off in Settings) and bug reports you choose to send (viewing the board, uploading two-hand scores, reporting, deleting your own data, the daily check-in, sending daily totals, sending a report).
  • Haptic feedback (vibration): needs no separate permission in iOS; it can be turned off in settings.

10. Children

The App is not designed specifically for children under 13. Leaderboard nicknames are shown publicly, so parents should tell children not to use real names; to have a minor's data deleted, use the in-app deletion (Settings > Delete all my data); if the App has already been deleted, we can identify the data only by the random player ID inside the App and cannot delete on the basis of an email; the data is deleted automatically 180 days after the last check-in or score upload, and a parent may still email mrjc0509@gmail.com and we will reply with an explanation. Apart from the leaderboard, player reports and any bug report you choose to send, the App does not collect anyone's personal data, including children's; parents should also remind children not to write personal data in a bug report description.
