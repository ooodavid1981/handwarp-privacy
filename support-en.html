Support

"HandWarp" uses the front camera to recognize your hands so you can steer a rocket in the air; all gesture recognition happens on your device.

What you need

  • An iPhone (played in landscape).
  • Camera permission: gesture control needs the front camera; without it you can still play with the on-screen touch buttons.

FAQ

  • How do I play with gestures: hold the phone in landscape and raise both hands in front of the camera. Both fists = accelerate; either hand open = brake; tilt your two hands like holding a steering wheel = turn left or right. It works best in good light with both hands in view.
  • Camera permission is off: go to iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera and turn on "HandWarp".
  • Gestures are not detected: make sure there is enough light, both hands are in view and the background is not too busy; you can also play with the on-screen touch buttons instead.
  • I do not want to use gestures: you can play with the on-screen buttons (the web version also supports the keyboard). Note: the online leaderboard accepts only two-hand gesture scores; touch and keyboard scores are not uploaded.
  • Leaderboard nickname: when you first submit a score you can choose a nickname (2 to 16 characters). It is shown publicly, so please do not use your real name. Nicknames are filtered for profanity, URLs and phone numbers.
  • Report an inappropriate nickname: in the App, Leaderboard > tap the player's entry > Report; or email mrjc0509@gmail.com. When a player's nickname receives unresolved nickname reports within 30 days from at least three players who have played and who are on three different networks, the system automatically hides that player's nickname first (the leaderboard then shows a default name; the score is not affected), and the developer then reviews it manually. The mask applies to the player, not to one nickname, so it stays on after a name change until the developer has reviewed it. When there are pending reports, the developer receives a daily notification. We never remove a player from the leaderboard automatically just because of reports.
  • Delete my data: in the App, Settings > Delete all my data deletes your nickname, scores, the reports you sent and made against you, rejected-submission records and the player data from the server in one step, and clears the game data on this device only after you are connected and the server has confirmed the deletion. If you have already deleted the App, we cannot confirm your identity from a nickname or score and cannot delete on the basis of an email; the data is deleted automatically 180 days after your last check-in or score upload. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Want to report a problem: in the App, Settings > Feedback > Report a problem lets you report it directly (choose a category and write a few words; no personal data needed).
  • Endless practice flight: no score, no time limit, you cannot crash; good for learning the gestures.
  • Are camera images uploaded: no. Images are analyzed on your phone in real time and are never stored or uploaded.
